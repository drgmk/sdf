Welcome to sdb

sdb collects target and photometry data, and sdf fits the exported photometry with stellar and disk models, including multiple stellar systems.

Browse public SEDs and samples. Sign in to access or create private samples and import new targets.

You are welcome to use results from this site. Please assess them critically, and ask if you need help.

The fitting method is described in Ben Yelverton's paper on binaries, 2019MNRAS.488.3588Y. Please cite it when appropriate.
